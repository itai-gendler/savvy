# Check readiness

Check that the meeting is ready with Check readiness, from Prepare, Your meeting brief or the Start Listening shortcut. Savvy checks microphone and meeting-audio permission, then the transcription key and reasoning CLI with your own providers, or the minutes available with Savvy managed. Permissions do not confirm signal or transcription; Check audio does.
